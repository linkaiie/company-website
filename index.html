How it works

No hardware, no new phone, no app for your customers. Your number stays the same; only the calls you miss are handled differently.

1

Tell us about your business

Register, then describe your shop in a sentence or two. Your opening hours, services and prices are set up from that. Takes about a minute.

2

Divert missed calls

You get a dedicated Irish number. Dial **61*<your AI number># on your mobile once. From then on, calls you don’t answer go to your receptionist instead of voicemail. Works on Eir, Vodafone, Three and Virgin Media.

3

Bookings appear in your calendar

The receptionist checks your live availability, offers times, books the slot and emails or texts you straight away. Every call and booking shows up in your dashboard with a one-line summary.

Questions

Anything else, email hello@linkai.ie and a person will answer.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Keep your existing number and set conditional call forwarding so unanswered calls go to your receptionist. We walk you through it in setup.

What happens when I do answer?

Nothing changes. The diversion only applies to calls you miss or decline — answer as normal and the receptionist never picks up.

Will callers know it’s not a person?

There’s an optional disclosure greeting (on by default, recommended under GDPR). The voice is a natural Irish accent; most callers simply make their booking.

What happens to the booking?

It goes straight into your LinkAI calendar, and you get an email (and optional SMS) with the customer’s name, number and the job.

Is my data safe?

No call recordings or full transcripts are stored — only a short summary. Customer phone numbers are masked, in line with Irish and EU GDPR rules.
